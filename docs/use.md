# Use SAM

Everything you need to run your AppImages in a sandbox: getting SAM onto your
computer, your first sandbox, and what each switch does. You never need the
command line, but everything also works there.

- **[Install SAM](install.md):** what your computer needs, installing the
  AppImage, and your first sandbox.
- **[User guide](guide.md):** the window, every setting group by group, and
  what to do when an app does not work.
- **[Permissions reference](permissions.md):** for the command line: every
  permission key, preset files, and changing several apps at once.
- **[What's new](../CHANGELOG.md):** what changed in each version of SAM,
  newest first.

## The short version

1. **Install SAM and open it.** It lists the AppImages in your usual folders.
2. **Look over the access.** Select an AppImage and check the access SAM
   recommends for it.
3. **Create the sandbox and launch.** Press *Create Sandbox*, then *Launch*.

Not sure an app gets what it needs? **Test**, next to *Launch*, checks from
inside the sandbox that every setting holds and that the app starts.
