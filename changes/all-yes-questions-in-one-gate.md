### Every change that waits for a yes goes through one gate

- SAM decided in several places, added one command at a time, when a
  change waits for a yes: a change rated *Think twice* or *Dangerous*,
  compatibility mode, a step from the app's own output and a protection
  turned off for every app. One gate now decides it for every command, and
  the window asks by the same rule. The refusals, their codes and their
  messages stay as they were.
- Two repair steps now carry the label of what they change, as the risk
  list rates it: *Allow nested namespaces* reads *Some access* instead of
  *Harmless*, and *Turn off the baseline syscall filter* is rated *Think
  twice* in its own definition, as SAM already showed it. When SAM asks
  first does not change.
