### An update's note of new needs stays until you have seen it

- When an update seemed to need more (the network, sound, a folder), SAM
  showed a note and kept your settings. Any change of the app's switches
  took the note away, also a change of another switch, a change for a
  group of apps on the permissions page, or one made while the app page
  hid the note under "changed outside SAM". The note was then gone
  without anyone having seen it.
- Now the note goes only with its own "Got It" (or the app's review), or,
  need by need, once the app's settings grant that need at the level the
  update asked for. Unpacking the app again keeps what an earlier update
  asked for and adds what is new. Found by the independent review of
  PR #122.
