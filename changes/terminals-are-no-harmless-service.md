### Terminals are no harmless service

- A terminal that runs any command it is sent (GNOME Terminal, Console,
  Ptyxis, Tilix, Konsole, Yakuake, Xfce Terminal, Guake) counts as
  Dangerous to reach over D-Bus: allowing it, or a function of it, asks
  first, and a repair step from the app's output no longer offers it as
  "Allow just this service". Apps that already have one allowed keep it;
  their page now shows the risk.
